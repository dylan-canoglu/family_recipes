  * **Ingredients:**
    * 300g flour
    * 150g specialized puff-pastry margarine or butter
    * 2 tablespoons oil
    * 50ml water
    * 1/2 teaspoon salt

**Original (French):**
  * **Préparation:** 30 min
  * **Repos de la pâte:** 2 h
  * **Cuisson:** Th. 8
  * **Pour:** 6 personnes
  * **Ingrédients:**

    * 150 g de margarine à feuilletage
    * 2 c. à soupe d'huile
    * 1/2 dl d'eau (1/2 verre)
    * 1/2 c. à café de sel

### Instructions (Recipe)
**English:**
  * **Directions:**
    1. Mix the flour, oil, water, and salt to form a smooth, soft dough (the "détrempe"). Rest for 30 minutes.
    2. Roll the dough into a rectangle. Place the softened butter in the center and fold the flaps of the dough over to completely seal the fat inside.
    3. Roll the enclosed dough into a long, neat rectangle. Fold it into thirds like a letter. Rotate the dough 90 degrees so the fold is on your right. Roll out and fold into thirds again. This constitutes 2 "turns". Let the dough rest in the fridge for 30 minutes.

    * Étaler la pâte en un long rectangle. Plier en 3 (comme une lettre). Tourner la pâte d'un quart de tour (le pli à droite). Étaler de nouveau et replier en 3. Cela fait les 2 premiers "tours". Laisser reposer 30 min au frais.
    * Répéter cette opération pour faire 6 tours au total, en observant des temps de repos au frais toutes les deux étapes. Utiliser la pâte bien froide. Cuire à four chaud.

- **Source Image:** IMG_6028.HEIC (Page/Image #45)
- **Estimated Time:** Prep: 15 mins | Cook: 20 mins | Total: 35 mins
- **Complexity Level:** Hard

### Ingredients
None listed in source.

### Instructions (Recipe)
* **Directions:**
    * Take out the foie gras 1 hour before working. Separate the two lobes, and delicately remove the veins (vein map: 2 layers of "Y" veins on top, and a lower vein).
    * Season with 16g of seasoning per kilo of liver. Seasoning mix: Sichuan pepper, black pepper, and salt, crushed in a mortar. Distribute the seasoning on both sides.
    * Place in a salad bowl, add 3cl of cognac, and let marinate in the fridge for 30 min - 1 hour.
    * Lay out a torchon (towel), place the liver on it, roll the liver inside the torchon tightly, and secure the ends of the torchon with butcher's twine.
    * Boil water, add coarse salt, and submerge the torchon in the boiling water. Let return to a boil and immediately turn off the heat (water must completely cover the torchon).
    * Leave the torchon in the hot water to finish cooking, then let cool to room temperature (a few hours).
    * Remove the foie gras from the water, tighten the torchon to squeeze out excess fat, tie it back up, and leave in the fridge for 12 hours.

    * **Garnish:** 4 slices of bread, 40g grated gruyere cheese, 20g margarine.

**Original (French):**
  * **Préparation:** 15 min
  * **Cuisson:** À la cocotte ordinaire: 20 min ; à l'autocuiseur: 10 min
  * **Gratin au gril:** quelques instants ; au four: 10 min (th. 8)
  * **Pour:** 4 personnes
  * **Ingrédients:**
    * 1 l d'eau
    * 125 g d'oignons
    * 40 g de beurre ou de margarine
    * 20 g de farine

    * **Garniture:** 4 tranches de pain, 40 g de gruyère râpé, 20 g de margarine.

### Instructions (Recipe)
**English:**

Cover and simmer for 20 minutes in a standard pot (or 10 minutes in a pressure cooker).
    2. Meanwhile, brown the bread slices in a pan or toast them in the oven.
